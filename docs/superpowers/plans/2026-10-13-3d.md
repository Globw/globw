# Easier v1.3: 3D Graphics — Implementation Plan

> Executed inline (superpowers:executing-plans), TDD.

**Goal:** Software 3D rendering into the existing window: camera, rotation, cubes, boxes, spheres and triangles, with near-plane clipping, back-face culling, flat shading and a depth buffer.

**Spec:** `docs/spec.md` §6.10.

## Design
- **`canvas.rs`:** `Canvas` gains `depth: Vec<f32>`, set to infinity by `new` and `clear`.
- **`render3d.rs`:**
  - **Types:** `Vec3` (f64) with sub, cross, dot and normalize; `Scene { cam_pos, turn, tilt, rot: (ax, ay, az) }`, with `Default` putting the eye at the origin looking towards +z.
  - **`Scene::to_camera(p)`** gives camera space: translate, then rotate by -turn around y, then by -tilt around x. In camera space x is right, y is up and z is forward.
  - **Projection:** `f = (h/2) / tan(30°)`, `sx = w/2 + x*f/z`, `sy = h/2 - y*f/z`.
  - **`Scene::triangle(canvas, a, b, c, color, two_sided)`**:
    1. transform to camera space;
    2. shade with the world-space normal: `light = normalize(-0.4, 0.8, -0.5)` (from the upper left and towards the viewer), `k = 0.35 + 0.65*max(0, n·light)` for one-sided triangles and `|n·light|` for two-sided ones;
    3. cull back faces (one-sided triangles whose camera-space normal faces away);
    4. clip against `z = 0.1` (Sutherland–Hodgman, which yields 0, 1 or 2 triangles);
    5. rasterize with barycentric coordinates over the bounding box, interpolate `1/z` for the depth test, and write color and depth.
  - **Shapes:** `cube` and `box` build 12 triangles, `sphere` builds a 12×16 latitude/longitude mesh. The vertices are rotated by `rot` (x, then y, then z) around the shape's center.
- **Builtins:**
  - **new commands:** `CAMERA(5 floats)`, `ROTATE(3 floats)`, `CUBE(4 floats, string)`, `BOX(6 floats, string)`, `SPHERE(4 floats, string)` and `TRIANGLE3D(9 floats, string)`;
  - **dispatch** goes through `Graphics.scene`;
  - **reset:** `WINDOW` resets the scene.
- **Editor:** the grammar colors the new names. The extension becomes 0.4.0.

## Tasks

   - `point_in_front_projects_to_center`;
   - `cube_in_front_covers_center`;
   - `nearer_shape_wins_whatever_the_order`;
   - `behind_camera_draws_nothing`;
   - `turning_the_camera_moves_the_view` (after turning 180° the cube in front is gone);
   - `crossing_the_near_plane_is_clipped` (a floor through the eye draws without panicking);
   - `shading_differs_between_faces`;
   - `clear_resets_depth`.
